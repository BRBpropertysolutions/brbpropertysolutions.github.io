Institutional Supported Housing Leasing

BRB Property Solutions provides professional and institutional landlords with a structured, long-term leasing model into supported social housing — delivering guaranteed rent, full operational management, and asset condition protection.

Built for Portfolio & Professional Landlords

This model is designed for landlords managing portfolios, investment vehicles, or long-term holdings who prioritise income stability, governance, and asset protection.

BRB Property Solutions leases properties directly, remains contractually responsible throughout the term, and manages the interface between housing partners, support providers, and local authorities.

While residents may change, the lease structure and rental income remain consistent — providing predictable cash-flow and reduced operational burden.

Key Commercial Benefits

Guaranteed Rental Income

Rent paid for the full lease term, independent of voids, arrears, or resident turnover.

Long-Term Lease Structures

Multi-year lease arrangements reduce churn and improve forward income visibility.

Full Operational Management

Management, inspections, compliance oversight, and coordination handled end-to-end.

Asset Condition Protection

Properties monitored through the term and returned in an agreed condition.

Single Point of Accountability

BRB Property Solutions remains responsible, reducing landlord exposure to operational risk.

Ethical, Council-Aligned Use

Homes used within supported housing pathways aligned with local authority frameworks.
