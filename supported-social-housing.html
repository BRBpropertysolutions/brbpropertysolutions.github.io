Supported Social Housing

BRB Property Solutions works with landlords to supply high-quality homes into supported social housing arrangements under long-term, professionally managed lease structures.

We provide guaranteed rent, full property management, and condition protection, allowing landlords to benefit from secure, hands-off income without day-to-day involvement.

What We Offer Landlords

Guaranteed Rent

Rent is paid for the full term of the lease, regardless of occupancy, void periods, or tenant circumstances. This provides predictable, reliable income without arrears risk.

Long-Term Lease Security

Properties are taken on under longer-term lease arrangements, reducing turnover and giving landlords certainty over future income.

Full Management Included

We handle all aspects of management, including tenant liaison, inspections, compliance, repairs coordination, and local authority engagement.

Property Condition Guaranteed

Properties are returned in an agreed condition at the end of the lease, with ongoing monitoring and maintenance carried out throughout the term.

Hands-Off Investment

Landlords are not involved in day-to-day issues. BRB Property Solutions acts as the single point of responsibility, allowing a truly passive income arrangement.

Ethical & Responsible Use

Homes are used to support vulnerable individuals through structured, regulated supported housing pathways, in partnership with councils and support providers.

Who This Is Suitable For

Supported social housing leasing is particularly suitable for:

• Landlords seeking long-term income stability
• Owners looking to reduce management involvement
• Portfolio landlords wanting predictable cash flow
• Investors prioritising ethical and socially responsible use
