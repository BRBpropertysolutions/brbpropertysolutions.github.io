Institutional Supported Housing Leasing

BRB Property Solutions provides professional and institutional landlords with a structured, long-term leasing model into supported social housing.

Our approach delivers guaranteed rent, operational certainty, and full asset management, removing volatility and day-to-day involvement while supporting ethical housing outcomes.

Designed for Portfolio & Professional Landlords

This leasing model is built specifically for landlords managing portfolios, investment vehicles, or long-term holdings who prioritise income stability, governance, and asset protection.

BRB Property Solutions leases properties directly, remains contractually responsible throughout the term, and manages the interface between housing partners, support providers, and local authorities.

While residents may change, the lease structure and rental income remain consistent, providing a predictable cash-flow profile comparable to other institutional leasing arrangements.

Key Commercial Benefits

Guaranteed Rental Income

Rent paid for the full lease term, independent of voids, arrears, or resident turnover.

Long-Term Lease Structures

Reduced churn and stable income over multi-year lease arrangements.

Full Operational Management

All management, inspections, compliance oversight, and coordination handled.

Asset Condition Protection

Properties monitored throughout the lease and returned in an agreed condition.

Single Point of Accountability

BRB Property Solutions remains responsible, reducing landlord exposure to operational risk.

Ethical, Council-Aligned Use

Homes are used within regulated supported housing pathways, aligned with local authority frameworks.
