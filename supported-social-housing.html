Frequently Asked Questions

Who is responsible for the property day-to-day?

BRB Property Solutions acts as the single point of responsibility, managing the property and liaising with housing partners.

Is rent paid if the property is empty?

Yes. Rent is guaranteed for the duration of the lease, regardless of occupancy.

What happens at the end of the lease?

The property is returned in an agreed condition, subject to the terms set out at the start of the lease.

What type of properties are suitable?

Typically self-contained homes with strong condition and compliance potential. We will confirm suitability after a quick review.

Is this compatible with lender or insurance requirements?

We encourage landlords to confirm any lender and insurance requirements before entering into an agreement. We provide clear documentation to support that process.
