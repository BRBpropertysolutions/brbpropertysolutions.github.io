BRB Property Solutions provides responsible, transparent, and compliant property solutions for landlords, investors, and local authority partners across the UK.

We specialise in professionally managed housing arrangements that prioritise stability, governance, and long-term partnerships.

For Landlords & Property Owners

Guaranteed Rent

We lease properties directly from landlords, providing predictable, contracted rental income for the duration of the agreement. Rent is paid regardless of void periods or occupancy changes.

Hands-Off Management

We manage day-to-day operations including inspections, compliance oversight, maintenance coordination, and housing partner liaison — reducing landlord involvement.

Professional & Compliant Use

Properties are supplied within structured housing frameworks with safeguarding, governance, and accountability embedded throughout the management process.

Long-Term Stability

Our agreements are designed for landlords seeking secure longer-term arrangements, reducing churn and providing income certainty.

Supported Social Housing Leasing

For suitable properties, we offer long-term leasing into supported social housing arrangements. This includes guaranteed rent, full management, and contractual protection of the property’s condition.
